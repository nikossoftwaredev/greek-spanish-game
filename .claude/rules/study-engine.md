# Study engine

## Persistence (localStorage, no backend)

- Three persisted Zustand stores: `vamos:progress:v1`, `vamos:homework:v1`, `vamos:settings:v1`. Each uses `skipHydration: true`; `Providers` calls `rehydrateStores()` once, and components that show persisted values gate on `useHydrated()` so the server HTML and the first client render always match.
- Changing the shape of a persisted store means bumping its `version` and writing a `migrate`, because real learners already have data. Content ids are part of that data: never rename or reuse one.
- `lib/backup.ts` exports and imports all three stores as one JSON file. Keep it in sync when a store gains a field (`snapshotProgress` lists every progress field, including `wordResults` and `lessonProgress`; the backup also carries the `sounds` and `lolaVoice` settings).
- An additive field with a default in `INITIAL` needs no migration: persist merges the defaults over old data. Renaming or reshaping a field does.

## Two books, one model

- Aula chapters live in `content/units/` (`unit-N.ts` is a `UnitBase`: everything but the vocabulary). **The only source of Aula vocabulary is the Klett glossary** (the «Γλωσσάριο» of the Greek Anexo, `data/Α1.pdf`): `unit-N-pages.ts` holds one `VocabGroup` per printed book page («Página 14», «Páginas 18 y 19» is `p18`) with the words in glossary order and the exercise number of each (`VocabItem.exercise`), and `content/index.ts` joins them into `Unit`. A word keeps the id it first had (progress is stored by it) even when the glossary moves it to another chapter, and has **one card in the whole book**: a word the glossary repeats stays in its first chapter (`validate:content` fails on two cards for one `vocabKey`). The «Más ejercicios» pages (141 and up, `section: "Más ejercicios"`, range in `UnitBase.pages.masEjercicios`) are a separate booklet that is not in the Aula PDF, so `isInAulaPdf` hides the «Βιβλίο» button for them. The Anexo's extra thematic boxes («ΑΞΙΟΛΟΓΙΟ», «ΛΕΞΙΛΟΓΙΟ») have no page and are not part of the vocabulary. The Gramática de uso lives in `content/gramatica/` as `GuUnit` (a `GrammarLesson` plus `number`, `page` and `exercisesEl`). Both kinds of grammar topic are `FlatLesson`s (`lib/content/selectors.ts`) with a **key**: `1:genero` for Aula, `gu:50` for a Gramática unit. Taught ticks, drill statistics (`<key>:<drillId>`), practice (`/play/grammar?lesson=<key>`, `?lessons=a,b`, `?taught=1`) and the checklist all use keys, so a new feature must work for both books.
- Homework shorthand is parsed in `lib/homework/parse.ts` ("50.2" is Gramática de uso unit 50, exercise 2; "κεφάλαιο 2" and "σελ. 30-32" are Aula) and planned in `lib/homework/plan.ts`. Extend those two files (and their cases) when the class invents a new way of writing homework.
- The numbered exercises of the Gramática de uso are never reproduced (copyright), but each one is **playable in the app**: `exercisesEl` paraphrases what it asks, and `content/gramatica/practice/gu-*.ts` (`GuPractice`, merged by `withPractice` in `content/gramatica/index.ts`) tags every drill of the unit with the exercise it practises (`Drill.exercise`, 1-based) and adds original `x1, x2...` drills, so every exercise has 4 to 6. `/play/grammar?ex=50.2` runs one exercise (`SessionConfig.exercise`), and finishing it ticks `doneExercises` and, through `lib/homework/finish-exercise.ts`, completes the homework task once all its exercises are ticked. `validate:content` fails when an exercise has fewer than 4 drills or a drill has no exercise, so a new unit or drill must be tagged.
- Order drills are auto-checked as soon as the placed words equal any accepted order (`solution` or an `alternatives` entry) or the learner has placed as many words as the longest accepted order. Alternatives may therefore have a different word count than the solution, but every word of an alternative must be among the tiles.

## Class progress (what the learner decides)

- Two independent filters narrow what the games draw from: the **class unit** (vocabulary and verbs up to that chapter) and the **taught-lessons checklist** (`taughtLessons`, `unitId:lessonId` keys, edited on `/grammar`). Grammar drills come from the ticked lessons when a session has no explicit `units` param (`useTaught`), and `/play/grammar?taught=1` practises exactly the ticked lessons. An explicit `units`, `lesson` or `pages` param always wins.
- Vocabulary can also be filtered to the words printed on given book pages (`pages` param, `VocabGroup.page`): this is what the per-page "Κάρτες" and "Παίξε" buttons use.

## Exercises and sessions

- **Every exercise and flashcard says where it comes from** (`Exercise.source`, built in `lib/game/sources.ts` and shown by `SourceBadge` in `PromptHeader` and on the card: "Aula · Κεφάλαιο 3 · σελ. 50", "Gramática de uso · Ενότητα 50 · άσκηση 50.2", with a «Βιβλίο» button to that page). The end screen lists the chapters of each book the round used. A new exercise builder must set `source`; the smoke test fails on an exercise without one.
- **Greek to Spanish is the hard direction, so it is easy to practise**: flashcards start with the Greek (`cardDirection` setting, default `el-es`, a labelled «ΕΛ → ES» button that remembers the choice), the typing game asks for the Spanish from the Greek meaning, and the vocabulary list has «Κάλυψε τα ισπανικά / τα ελληνικά» to test yourself (the notes stay hidden while Spanish is covered, they can contain the answer).
- **Two typing-and-building vocabulary games** besides «Γράψε τη λέξη»: `translate` («Μετάφραση») asks in both directions, "how do you say X in Spanish?" (`elToEsType`) and "what does Y mean?" typed in Greek (`esToElType`, `TypeExercise.answerLang: "el"`, accepted forms from `greekForms`, Greek articles ignored, the Spanish special-character keys hidden), and `scramble` («Φτιάξε τη λέξη») builds a Spanish word from its shuffled letters (an `OrderExercise` with `letters: true`, single words of 3 to 10 letters). Both set `source` and rate the vocabulary card like any vocabulary exercise; `elToEsType` also accepts the Spanish of other words with the same Greek meaning.
- **Flashcards have two buttons**, «Δεν το ήξερα» (`again`) and «Το ήξερα» (`good`), not four.

- **The Greek translation under an exercise sentence is always visible** (the learner wants it there). Lola's speech (praise, insults, verdicts) and the lesson examples keep their Greek behind a «Μετάφραση» button (`components/translation.tsx`), so the Spanish is read first; the `showTranslations` setting shows those too. A new place that shows Lola's Greek or a lesson translation must use `Translation`; the task itself (the Greek sentence of an order drill, vocabulary meanings, reference pages) is never hidden.

- **Sound is a short effect and Lola's voice, both free and local, both optional.** `lib/sound.ts` plays a two-note "ding" with the Web Audio API (no files) from `playCorrect()` on a right answer in games, lesson exercises and the flashcard «Το ήξερα» (setting `sounds`, default on). `lib/voice.ts` reads Lola's Spanish with `speechSynthesis` (setting `lolaVoice`, default on) **only with a Spanish voice that is not a known man's** (`voiceAvailable`): the first version read Spanish with whatever voice existed and mispronounced it, which is why audio was removed once, and a later one took the first `es-ES` voice of the phone, which was a man. Voices are scored: a woman's name, Latin American first (`es-CO`, then `es-MX`, `es-US`) over Castilian, natural or online voices over local ones, a known man's name far below. The learner can pick a voice by hand in the settings (`VoicePicker`, setting `lolaVoiceName`, with a test button; device specific, so it is not in the backup). With no usable voice she stays silent and the «Άκουσέ τη» button is not shown. Her voice is never cloned from a real person (a celebrity's included): she is an original character. Never read Spanish with a non-Spanish voice, and never add listening games or per-word speak buttons without real recordings.

- Answers are checked **without a "Check" button** wherever the answer is unambiguous: a tapped option in a choice exercise is final, a typed answer that matches exactly is accepted 450 ms after the last key (an accent-less or wrong answer waits for Enter or the "Έλεγχος" button), and an ordered sentence is checked when its last word is placed. Every exercise view finishes by calling `onComplete(result)`; `onReady(evaluator)` is only for the manual button. A new exercise kind must follow the same rule.

- Randomness lives only in the builders (`lib/game/builders.ts`) and `shuffle`. React 19's purity lint forbids `Math.random()` and `Date.now()` during render, and `setState` inside an effect is an error: build a session with a lazy `useState(() => ...)` and restart a round by remounting with a new `key`.
- Typed answers go through `matchAnswer()`: leading articles are ignored, a missing accent is `accent` (correct, with a reminder), a missing `ñ` is wrong. Every new exercise kind must work with it and with the footer's "I don't know" button (`solutionOf`).
- `pnpm smoke:game` builds thousands of exercises and checks each can be answered. Run it after touching builders or content.

## Step-by-step lessons and Lola

- `/play/learn?lesson=<key>` (`mode: "learn"`, `components/learn/`) teaches one grammar lesson of either book as a flow: intro, each rule on its own screen with tappable examples, a "rebuild the example" check after the rule (`exampleOrder`), the tables and tips, a 4-drill quiz, and a verdict. The steps are built by the pure `buildLessonSteps` (`lib/game/lesson-steps.ts`); exercise steps reuse `ExerciseView` and `SessionFooter`, so auto-check and feedback behave exactly as in a game. Both lesson pages carry the «Μάθε το βήμα βήμα» button.
- **Lola** (`components/mascot/lola.tsx`) is the mascot: one original comic-style SVG (navy hair with blue streaks, big gold hoops, choker, yellow off-shoulder top, teal jacket, jeans) with six moods that change her face and pose: `teaching` (talking), `happy`, `wink`, `proud`, `annoyed`, `angry`. `body="bust"` is the top of the same drawing, so there is a single drawing to maintain. Colours are the `--lola-*` tokens in `globals.css` (the one place illustration colours are allowed). She plays the learner's Latina girlfriend, as a joke (admires and flirts when he is right, yells like a girlfriend at the end of her patience when he is wrong). Her lines live in `lib/mascot/lines.ts`, each with the mood she makes while saying it (rotated by position, no randomness): praise is flirty and smug, scolding is furious and swears **in Spanish only, with no Greek line** (`¡Joder, otra vez mal!`, `¡Qué burro!`: the learner asked for it, and the words are tappable so it doubles as vocabulary) but never attacks who the learner is. The `mascot` setting turns her off. Never base her on a real person's likeness.
- **She is on screen all the time, she can be dragged, and she talks** through one global `LolaCompanion` (`components/mascot/lola-companion.tsx`, rendered once in `components/providers.tsx`). Her position is a fraction of the free screen stored in the `lolaPosition` setting, so it survives navigation, reload and a different window size; a drag moves her with a transform and a tap (under 6 px and 600 ms) opens Ask Lola. Without a stored position she stands in the bottom corner, and on game screens right on top of the footer (`PlayShell` publishes the footer height to `lib/stores/lola-store.ts`, so her bubble never covers the exercise). What she says is not drawn by the screens: a screen calls `useLolaStore().say(line)` (the answer feedback in `SessionFooter`, the lesson steps in `lesson-player.tsx`, the verdict in `SessionSummary`, a greeting per page from `lolaGreeting`) and the companion shows it in a `SpeechBubble` (`components/mascot/speech-bubble.tsx`: tones `calm`, always cream with dark ink in both themes (the `lola-bubble` utility), and `loud`, red and shaking; tails `left`, `left-low`, `right`, `down`, `down-left`) on whichever side has room, shrunk to fit, so it never runs off the screen. A line with an empty `es` is a Greek coaching remark and is shown as plain text. A new screen that wants her to comment calls `say`; it never draws a second Lola.
- **Ask Lola** (`components/mascot/ask-lola-dialog.tsx`, key `ASK_LOLA_DIALOG`; open it with the header button, a tap on the floating Lola, or `openDialog(ASK_LOLA_DIALOG)`): the learner types Greek (or a Spanish phrase) and she answers. `askLocal` (`lib/lola/ask.ts`) answers offline from `content/phrasebook.ts` (about 100 hand-written phrases, matched ignoring accents, punctuation and wrappers like «πώς λέμε ... στα ισπανικά», plus «τι σημαίνει ...») and from the book vocabulary; only when that is not `confident` does `translateText` (`lib/lola/translate.ts`) send **the typed text and nothing else** to MyMemory, cached in `vamos:ask:v1`, and the answer is labelled as machine translation. Keep that boundary: never send anything else about the learner, and add a phrase to the phrasebook (smoke test checks every wording finds its own phrase) instead of trusting a machine translation for common phrases. Keydown inside her panel must `stopPropagation`, because games and lessons listen for Enter on `window`.

## The game shell, progress and the guided path

- **No `position: fixed` bars inside animated or transformed parents.** Full-screen games, lessons and flashcards live in `PlayShell` (`components/play/play-shell.tsx`): `h-dvh flex-col`, a header with the exit button and progress (`shrink-0`, `safe-top`), a scrolling `main`, and a footer slot filled with `<PlayFooter>` (a React portal). The bars are part of normal flow, so in an installed PWA, with a mobile address bar or a software keyboard, the top and bottom stay locked and only the middle scrolls. Keyframes end in `transform: none` (a leftover transform makes the element the containing block of fixed descendants, which once left a lesson footer floating mid-screen on a real phone).
- **Progress is derived from two small maps** in the progress store: `wordResults` (was the last answer for this vocabulary id right) and `lessonProgress` (`{step, total, done}` per lesson key). `lib/game/progress.ts` turns them into the numbers: a word is learned when its last answer was right, a drill when it was answered right at least once and right at least as often as wrong, `vocabProgress` / `grammarProgress` per chapter (the chapter's Aula lessons plus the Gramática units mapped to it by `gramaticaRefsForUnit`), and `nextStepForUnit` gives the single next thing to do (unknown vocabulary first, then grammar drills, then the next chapter). The chapter and grammar pages show it in a sticky bar (`components/progress/sticky-progress.tsx`), the home and chapter buttons say where they lead, and an end screen's «Επόμενο» pushes the same href with a fresh `&r=` marker so `PlayClient` (which keys players by the query string) starts a new round. Flashcards: «Δεν το ήξερα» cards go back to the end of the deck until answered «Το ήξερα», and a `focus=1` round takes 12 unknown words (missed ones first).
- **Lessons remember where the learner stopped**: `LessonPlayer` saves the step on every advance (`setLessonStep`, `completeLesson`), reopens at that step with a notice and «Από την αρχή», and a finished lesson offers «Μόνο το τεστ».
- **Installing**: `public/sw.js` (a pass-through service worker, registered only in production), `lib/pwa.ts` (captures `beforeinstallprompt`; `installMode()` is `hidden` when already installed or on a desktop, `prompt`, `ios` or `manual`) and `InstallBanner` / `InstallButton` with the instructions dialog. Never make the service worker cache app pages: a stale shell would hide new content from the learner.

## Spaced repetition

- FSRS through `ts-fsrs` (`lib/srs/fsrs.ts`), one card per vocabulary id, with empty learning steps (a session already repeats wrong answers). Game answers rate a card once per session: wrong is `again`, right is `good`, a missing accent is `hard`. Flashcards use all four ratings.
- Mastery (`new`, `learning`, `known`, `mastered`) is derived from FSRS stability. Do not store it.

## Tap-a-word lookup

- `lookupLocal` (book vocabulary, verb forms, function words) answers instantly and offline. `lookupRemote` adds Wiktionary (lemma, form description, definitions, example) and MyMemory (Greek machine translation, only when the book has no Greek). Both are free, key-less and CORS-open, and are called straight from the browser. Only the tapped word leaves the device. Every answer is cached in `vamos:lookup:v1`.
- Never send anything else about the learner to these services, and do not add another third-party request without recording it in the README.

## Next.js gotchas hit in this project

- Do not import a non-component value (a constant, a map) from a `"use client"` module into a server component: it arrives as a client reference, not as the value. Shared constants live in plain modules (`lib/game/modes.ts`).
- Do not use `@theme inline` variables inside `var()` in custom CSS: `inline` does not emit them. Reference the font variable (`--font-alegreya`) directly.
- Vocabulary carries **no emoji**, on purpose (the learner asked for it to be removed everywhere): there is no `emoji` field on words or groups and no picture-matching exercise. Do not add them back.

## Copyright
